How we rank Enshrouded builds

Every build on this list can finish Enshrouded 1.0. For what Update 1.0 changed on October 15, 2026 and how to re-spec, read the Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide. The tiers measure how efficient and forgiving a build is at the new level 50 cap with gear up to level 55. We score four things: damage output, survivability, solo strength and ease of use. A-tier builds are not weak. Several of them, like the Tank and Healer, are more valuable than S-tier builds in a coordinated co-op group.

S-tier Enshrouded builds

The Battlemage is the build almost every 1.0 guide ranks first: Unity and Arcane Deflection keep its mana nearly infinite, and a ward or shield covers mistakes. The Necromancer (summoner) joins it in S-tier because Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery let skeletons and totems hold whole fights. The Wizard has the best area damage in the game thanks to the new 1.0 spells and Blast and Cast. The Barbarian brings the highest physical damage, using two-handed stuns as defense.

A-tier Enshrouded builds

The Tank and Healer rank lower only because they shine in groups. The Warrior is the best build for beginners. The Ranger improved a lot with Eternal arrows and Puncture.

B-tier Enshrouded builds

The Assassin has the biggest single hit in the game: Sneak Attack deals +900% damage to unaware enemies, and daggers now live in its own tree. But sneak attacks are hard to land on bosses and the build has little armor, so most 1.0 guides rank it near the bottom. It is fun and strong in skilled hands.
