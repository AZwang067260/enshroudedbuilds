Enshrouded Builds Tier List (1.0)

The best Enshrouded builds ranked for the 1.0 full release, based on damage, survivability, solo strength and how forgiving each build is.

S

Wizard

Best area damage in the game. New 1.0 spells add crowd control.

Battlemage

Near-infinite mana, shield safety. Best solo build.

Barbarian

Highest physical DPS; stuns double as defense.

A

Necromancer

Brand new in 1.0. Relaxed and strong solo, still settling.

Healer

Top value in groups, weak alone.

Warrior

Most forgiving build with strong self-sustain.

Tank

Essential in co-op and nearly unkillable. Slower solo.

Ranger

Eternal arrows and new skills; aim-dependent.

Assassin

Highest crit burst, but fragile and hard to play.

How we rank Enshrouded builds

Every build on this list can finish Enshrouded 1.0. The tiers measure how efficient and forgiving a build is at the new level 50 cap with gear up to level 55. We score four things: damage output, survivability, solo strength and ease of use. A-tier builds are not weak. Several of them, like the Tank and Healer, are more valuable than S-tier builds in a coordinated co-op group.

S-tier Enshrouded builds

The Wizard keeps the top spot thanks to unmatched area damage and the new 1.0 spells. The Battlemage is the best solo build: Unity and Arcane Deflection keep its mana nearly infinite and the shield covers mistakes. The Barbarian brings the highest physical damage, using two-handed stuns as defense.

A-tier Enshrouded builds

The Tank and Healer rank lower only because they shine in groups. The Warrior is the best build for beginners. The Ranger improved a lot with Eternal arrows, and the Assassin benefits from daggers moving into its tree. The Necromancer may rise to S-tier once 1.0 hotfixes settle the summon numbers.

Tier lists change with patches. This list was last reviewed on October 17, 2026.
