How to Summon Skeletons and Totems in Enshrouded 1.0

The skeleton summon is a spell called Hollow Wretch, crafted by the Collector. The Fire, Ice and Shock totems come from the Alchemist. You can keep 1 of each kind out by default and up to 4 with two skills. Here is every summon spell, its recipe and how to raise the cap.

Spells marked Eternal don't use charges: you craft them once and keep them. The changelog says every spell in 1.0 has an Eternal version, and Eternal spells can be upgraded like weapons.

How to get the skeleton spell (Hollow Wretch)

All Hollow Wretch spells are crafted by the Collector. The first one only needs Bones and Ectoplasm Fragments, which Hollow enemies drop in the Springlands and Revelwood Hollow Halls:

Hollow Wretch ICollector-20 Ectoplasm Fragment, 50 Bones100 charges

A Hollow Wretch is an ally that attacks enemies with Cutting and Ice damage and stays for 60 to 120 seconds depending on the tier. Its mana cost is high (102 for the first tier), so cast it before the fight starts.

Which totem to use

  • Fire Totem: the highest damage per tier, and it explodes when it expires. Best for damage.
  • Ice Totem: its projectiles slow enemies by 50% for 5 seconds, so enemies take longer to reach you.
  • Shock Totem: the cheapest totem to cast, and its hits deal Stamina damage that fills the enemy's stun bar. The Stamina damage scales with your Intelligence.

Totems last 8 to 11 seconds, so you recast them often. With Totem Mastery you can have several out at once, and you can mix types, for example Ice to slow and Fire to damage.

How many skeletons and totems you can have

Every summon spell says the maximum is "1 by default which can be increased via skills". Two skills raise it, one rank at a time. The text below is the in-game text from the 1.0 game files (build 1267041):

SkillCostRank 1Rank 2Rank 3
Advanced Necromancy2 pt per rankIncreases the maximum amount of summoned Hollow allies from spells by 1.Increases the maximum amount of summoned Hollow allies from spells by 2.Increases the maximum amount of summoned Hollow allies from spells by 3.
Totem Mastery2 pt per rankIncreases the maximum amount of summoned totems by 1.Increases the maximum amount of summoned totems by 2.Increases the maximum amount of summoned totems by 3.

So the maximum is 4 Hollow Wretches and 4 totems. From an empty tree, maxing Advanced Necromancy costs 10 points by the cheapest path and Totem Mastery 11. On top of that, 1.0 added a global limit: the game "counts the currently active creatures spawned by players and enforces a maximum. When players cast more creatures than the allowed maximum, the oldest creature gets replaced."

Other ways to get minions

  • Necromancer skill (1 pt per rank, 3 ranks): When killing an enemy with a Magic weapon, you have a 15% chance to summon a friendly Skull Companion.
  • Gem of Lesser Necromancy: a weapon gem that has a chance to raise an unarmored Hollow ally when you kill an enemy, if you don't already have one. The wiki says it drops from the Hollow Reaper in the Nomad Highlands Hollow Halls. 1.0 gave it a limit on how many companions it can raise, and gem companions now "start to lose health after a while when out of combat."

Summon spells FAQ

How do you summon skeletons in Enshrouded?

Cast the Hollow Wretch spell from a staff. Hollow Wretch I is a level 17 spell crafted by the Collector from 20 Ectoplasm Fragment and 50 Bones, which gives 100 charges.

What is the skeleton summon spell called in Enshrouded?

Hollow Wretch. There are four versions: Hollow Wretch I and II, and Eternal Hollow Wretch I and II, which never run out of charges.

How many skeletons can you summon in Enshrouded?

1 by default. Advanced Necromancy adds up to 3 more (2 points per rank), for 4 Hollow allies from spells.

How many totems can you have out at once?

1 by default. Totem Mastery adds up to 3 more (2 points per rank), for 4 totems.

Where do you get totem spells in Enshrouded?

The Alchemist crafts them. Tier I totems are made at an Alchemy Station; the higher tiers and Eternal totems need a Laboratory.

Is there a limit on summons in Enshrouded 1.0?

Yes. Besides the per-spell caps, the 1.0 changelog says the game counts the active creatures spawned by players and enforces a maximum: when you go over it, the oldest creature is replaced.
