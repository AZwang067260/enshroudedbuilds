Enshrouded 1.0 Healer / Support Build

The player every co-op group wants. Keep the frontline standing and still add damage with your wand.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 healer build (also called the support build) puts points into Intelligence and Spirit, uses healing staff + wand and follows this skill order to level 50: Healer → Water Aura → Wand Mastery → Waters of Life → Healing Revive → Saviour → Unity. Rated A-tier after the Update 1.0 skill tree reset (October 15, 2026).

Quick answer

Healer: A-tier, medium difficulty, best for co-op (2–16 players). It scales with Intelligence and Spirit and uses healing staff + wand. The level 50 planner preset spends 212 of 212 points. Core skills: Healer, Water Aura, Waters of Life, Unity, Life Burst (+8 more). Main weakness: weak solo.

Enshrouded Healer build overview

The Enshrouded Healer build is a co-op support build. Enshrouded supports up to 16 players on a server, and group content in 1.0 is tuned to punish parties that cannot recover from big hits. A dedicated healer, using healing staves and the Healer section of the Mage tree, keeps tanks and melee DPS alive and adds wand damage between heals.

This support build focuses on healing output, mana sustain and staying out of danger. It also uses the new 1.0 utility spells: Entangle roots enemies off your allies and Ice Totems slow down packs.

What changed for the Healer in Enshrouded 1.0

Bosses at the new level 50 cap and gear 55 tier hit much harder, and groups without healing burn through food and potions. A support player cuts down on wipes and lets the Tank and Barbarian play aggressively.

Because of the 1.0 skill tree reset, many returning players are trying support for the first time. Healer skills share sustain nodes (Unity, Life Burst) with the Battlemage, so it is easy to hybridize.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Healer after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Healer, Water Aura, Intelligence + Spirit nodes.
  3. Put attribute points into Intelligence, Spirit, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Healing staff + wand) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

How to play the Healer build

  1. Position behind the tank, with a clear line of sight to the whole group.
  2. Weave wand shots between heals to proc Unity.
  3. Heal before a boss's big attacks, not after.
  4. Use Entangle on any enemy that dives your backline.
  5. Call out low mana in voice chat so the group can back off.

Enshrouded Healer build FAQ

Is a Healer build worth it in Enshrouded?

In co-op, yes. Most 1.0 guides rank it A-tier as the best co-op support. In solo, a Battlemage with Life Burst gives you most of the sustain with much more damage.

What weapon does a Healer use in Enshrouded?

A healing staff for healing, plus a wand for damage and Unity mana procs.

How many players can play Enshrouded together?

Enshrouded supports up to 16 players on a server. A healer becomes more valuable the bigger the group.
