Enshrouded 1.0 Assassin (Dagger & Crit) Build

Daggers moved to the Assassin tree in 1.0. Get in, crit and get out before the Shroud catches up.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 assassin build puts points into Dexterity and Endurance, uses daggers + bow and follows this skill order to level 50: Dagger Mastery → Sneak Attack → Dagger Specialization → Backstab Mastery → Cutthroat → Expose Weakness → Graceful Stride. Rated B-tier after the Update 1.0 skill tree reset (October 15, 2026).

Quick answer

Assassin: B-tier, hard difficulty, best for solo. It scales with Dexterity and Endurance and uses daggers + bow. The level 50 planner preset spends 211 of 212 points. Core skills: Sneak Attack, Cutthroat, Expose Weakness, Slice And Dice, Survival Instinct (+9 more). Main weakness: very fragile.

Enshrouded Assassin build overview

The Enshrouded Assassin build is all about critical hits with bows and daggers, plus better glider efficiency for moving around the map. The biggest 1.0 change for this build is that dagger skills moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree. For the first time, a dagger Assassin can put all of its points in one part of the skill tree.

This 1.0 Assassin guide pairs daggers for close-range crit bursts with a bow for openers. It covers the new crit-based skill path, why Survival Instinct works so well for Shroud exploration, and how to survive in light armor.

What changed for the Assassin in Enshrouded 1.0

Before 1.0, dagger builds had to split points between Warrior and Ranger. With daggers in the Assassin section, crit and dagger nodes are close together, so the build becomes stronger much earlier.

Survival Instinct also counts dagger kills, so Assassins can stay longer in the Shroud than almost any other build.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Assassin after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Dexterity nodes, Dagger Mastery, Sneak Attack (Core).
  3. Put attribute points into Dexterity, Endurance, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Daggers + bow) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

How to play the Assassin build

  1. Open with a charged bow shot on the priority target.
  2. Close in during its stagger and dump a dagger combo.
  3. Dodge out before the counterattack. Never stand still.
  4. Pick off isolated enemies first. Avoid fighting a whole group in melee.
  5. Use the glider to reposition and to escape.

Enshrouded Assassin build FAQ

Where are the dagger skills in Enshrouded 1.0?

They moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree in 1.0.

Is the Assassin build good in 1.0?

It is fun and strong for skilled solo players, but most 1.0 tier lists, ours included, rank it B-tier: sneak attacks are hard to land on bosses and light armor is fragile.

Daggers or bow for the Assassin?

Both. Use the bow for openers and the daggers for burst on staggered enemies.
