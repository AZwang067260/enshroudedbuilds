Enshrouded Barbarian build overview

The Enshrouded Barbarian build is the game's melee damage build. The Barbarian archetype is designed around heavy two-handed weapons that stun enemies and deal huge damage. Push it toward the aggressive "Berserker" setup and you get what many 1.0 guides call the highest physical DPS in the game.

This guide covers a two-handed Barbarian for 1.0. It stacks Strength, takes the Barbarian archetype nodes, adds Brute, Blood Rage and Merciless Attack from the Warrior side, and adds enough Constitution to trade hits with bosses. You'll get the full skill order, the best two-handed weapons, armor priorities and a stun-lock rotation.

What changed for the Barbarian in Enshrouded 1.0

In 1.0, buffs to existing melee nodes and lower skill costs mean the Barbarian reaches its damage spikes earlier. Gear tier 55 also increases the base damage of top two-handers, and the Barbarian benefits most from big weapon numbers.

Two-handers have no shield, so survivability comes from killing fast and staggering enemies before they can attack. Stun is your defense.

1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset at the 1.0 launch on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept.

How to play the Barbarian build

  1. Open with a charged heavy attack to stun the most dangerous enemy.
  2. Chain heavy attacks on staggered targets. Merciless Attack shines here.
  3. Dodge out before your stamina runs out. Never empty the bar completely.
  4. On bosses, punish recovery windows only. Don't trade blow for blow.
  5. In co-op, attack from the side or behind while your Tank holds aggro.

Barbarian build pros and cons

Pros

  • Highest physical damage
  • Stuns are built-in crowd control
  • Very satisfying to play
  • No mana to manage

Cons

  • No shield
  • Stamina-hungry
  • Slow swings punish bad timing

Common barbarian build mistakes

  • Swinging until stamina is empty
  • Taking Vigorous Deflection (it needs a shield)
  • Ignoring Constitution

Enshrouded Barbarian build FAQ

What is the best melee build in Enshrouded 1.0?

For raw damage, the two-handed Barbarian (Berserker variant). For safety, the sword-and-shield Warrior.

Best two-handed weapon for the Barbarian?

Use the highest-level two-hander you have. Hammers stun best and swords swing fastest.

Is the Barbarian good for beginners?

It is fine, but harder than the Warrior because you have no shield. Learn the dodge first.
