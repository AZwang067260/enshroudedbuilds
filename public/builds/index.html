Choosing between Enshrouded builds

Because Enshrouded has no locked classes, every build here is a starting point, not a cage. Most players settle on one main archetype and borrow a few nodes from a neighboring one. A Warrior might take Tank armor scaling, a Wizard might add Battlemage wand sustain, and a Ranger might take Assassin crits. Each build page ends with a Hybrid builds section that shows which combinations work well.

If you are coming back after Update 1.0 (October 15, 2026), remember that your skill points were refunded. It is the best time to try something new, since you have to re-spend everything anyway. Our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide covers every change and the re-spec checklist.
