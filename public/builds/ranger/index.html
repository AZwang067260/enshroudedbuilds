Enshrouded Ranger build overview

The Enshrouded Ranger build is the main bow and archery build. The Ranger archetype focuses on bows, extra arrows and bonus damage to enemy weak points, so a good archer can kill most things before they even get close. 1.0 is a big update for archers: upgradeable Eternal arrows that cost mana instead of ammo, and three new skills (Puncture, Barrage and Survival Instinct).

This 1.0 Ranger guide covers the bow skill order, Dexterity priorities, which Eternal arrows to upgrade first, and how to stay alive at range. It also covers hybrid paths into the Assassin tree for crit-heavy players.

What changed for the Ranger in Enshrouded 1.0

Before 1.0, archers had to worry about arrow crafting. Eternal arrows remove most of that and add elemental options. Puncture increases ranged piercing damage, Barrage gives bow hits a chance to restore durability, and Survival Instinct extends your Shroud timer when you kill Shroud enemies with a bow or dagger.

Keen Games' official combat post left Puncture's value as a placeholder. Community planners built from the 1.0 game files list +10% piercing damage per rank (30% at max) for Puncture and a 15% chance per rank to restore 1 durability for Barrage. Check the in-game tooltip to be sure.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Ranger after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Ranger, Marksman, Dexterity nodes.
  3. Put attribute points into Dexterity, Constitution, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Bow + Eternal arrows) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

Key Ranger skills explained

Puncture

New in 1.0: up to +30% ranged piercing damage. The core damage skill.

Barrage

New in 1.0: durability back on hits, so long expeditions need fewer repairs.

Survival Instinct

New in 1.0: more Shroud time from kills. Great for exploring the Shroud.

Skill Shot

Up to +60% weak-point damage. Combined with Marksman and Puncture, headshots carry the build.

How to play the Ranger build

  1. Open from max range with a charged shot on a weak point.
  2. Switch arrow types by enemy instead of using one for everything.
  3. Back off with dodges and the glider. Height is your friend.
  4. Use Survival Instinct to stay longer in deep Shroud zones.
  5. In co-op, focus fire on the target your Tank is holding.

Enshrouded Ranger build FAQ

What are Eternal arrows in Enshrouded 1.0?

Upgradeable arrows added in 1.0 that cost mana instead of ammo. The fire line (Eternal Ember I, II and III) unlocks at levels 15, 28 and 45, and there is a shock line as well.

What are the new archer skills in Enshrouded 1.0?

Puncture (up to +30% ranged piercing damage), Barrage (up to 45% chance to restore 1 durability on bow hits) and Survival Instinct (up to +9 s Shroud time when you kill Shroud enemies with a bow or dagger).

Is the Ranger good solo?

Yes, especially in open areas. Keep a backup melee weapon or dagger for tight dungeons.
