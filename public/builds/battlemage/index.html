Enshrouded Battlemage build overview

The Enshrouded Battlemage build, which many players just call the wand build, mixes ranged magic with close-range survivability. You fire wand projectiles nonstop and keep a shield ready to parry, and your mana refills on its own through Unity and Arcane Deflection. You never really run out of resources, which is why many players think it is the best solo build in Enshrouded.

In 1.0, the Battlemage keeps its core skills (Wand Mastery, Sting, Unity, Life Burst) and gains new options. Wand Evasion Attack fires wider, piercing projectiles, Aerial Wand Attack adds an area attack from the air, and Blast and Cast lets you add a staff for burst. What changed for the Battlemage in Enshrouded 1.0

The 1.0 full release rewards hybrid players. Wand-focused characters got two new wand attacks and a crit-based bridge to staves. The new mage spells, especially Entangle and the summon totems, give the Battlemage crowd control it never had in early access.

The Battlemage holds up better than the pure Wizard because shield parries refund mana and Life Burst adds sustain. You can push into Shroud zones and fight elite packs alone without a healer.

1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset at the 1.0 launch on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept.

Key Battlemage skills explained

Wand Mastery

Chance for extra projectiles. The single biggest wand damage increase.

Unity

Mana on wand hits. This turns the wand into a perpetual-motion weapon.

Arcane Deflection

Rewards shield parries with mana and works well with a shield in the off-hand.

Wand Evasion Attack

One of the new 1.0 skills: piercing, wider shots that clear lines of enemies.

How to play the Battlemage build

  1. Start at mid range and hold the attack. Wand Mastery and Chain Hit do the work.
  2. When an elite swings, parry with the shield for Arcane Deflection mana.
  3. Drop a totem or Entangle on packs before engaging.
  4. Use Wand Evasion Attack after a dodge to punish groups that line up.
  5. Fight from the air with the glider and Aerial Wand Attack when enemies swarm.

Battlemage build pros and cons

Pros

  • Near-infinite mana
  • Very strong solo
  • Easy to learn
  • Scales well into 1.0 endgame

Cons

  • Lower burst than a pure Wizard
  • Parry timing needed for maximum value
  • Damage depends on wand level

Common battlemage build mistakes

  • Not upgrading the wand. Wand level is your DPS.
  • Skipping Constitution because the build "is a mage".
  • Ignoring the new 1.0 wand skills after the reset.

Enshrouded Battlemage build FAQ

Is the Battlemage wand build good in Enshrouded 1.0?

Yes. It is one of the best solo builds in 1.0. Unity and Arcane Deflection keep your mana topped up, and the new wand skills added more area damage.

Wand or staff for a Battlemage?

The wand is your main weapon. Carry a staff only if you plan to use Blast and Cast for burst windows.

Do I need a shield on a Battlemage?

It is strongly recommended. A shield enables parries, and Arcane Deflection restores mana on a successful parry.

What level do I get Unity?

Plan to reach it in the mid-game (around levels 16–35 in this path). The exact point depends on how many attribute nodes you take along the way.
