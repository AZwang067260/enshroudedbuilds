Enshrouded 1.0 Battlemage (Wand Build) Build

Endless wand fire with a ward or shield on your arm. The easiest strong solo build in Enshrouded 1.0.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 battlemage build puts points into Intelligence and Constitution, uses wand + ward or shield and follows this skill order to level 50: Mage Apprentice → Wand Specialization → Wand Mastery → Arcane Proliferation → Sting → Unity → Arcane Deflection. Rated S-tier after the Update 1.0 skill tree reset (October 15, 2026).

Quick answer

Battlemage: S-tier, easy–medium difficulty, best for solo. It scales with Intelligence and Constitution and uses wand + ward or shield. The level 50 planner preset spends 212 of 212 points. Core skills: Wand Mastery, Unity, Arcane Deflection, Wand Evasion Attack, Mage Apprentice (+11 more). Main weakness: lower burst than a pure Wizard.

Enshrouded Battlemage build overview

The Enshrouded Battlemage build, which many players just call the wand build, mixes ranged magic with close-range survivability. You fire wand projectiles nonstop and keep a ward or shield ready to parry, and your mana refills on its own through Unity and Arcane Deflection. You never really run out of resources, which is why many players think it is the best solo build in Enshrouded.

In 1.0, the Battlemage keeps its core skills (Wand Mastery, Sting, Unity, Life Burst) and gains new options. Wand Evasion Attack fires a piercing disc when you dodge, Aerial Wand Attack turns a mid-air shot into an explosion, and Blast and Cast lets you add a staff for burst. Keen Games also gave every wand a heavy attack in 1.0 that pierces enemies and can interrupt weaker ones. Below is the full 1.0 Battlemage skill order, attribute plan, gear list and rotation.

What changed for the Battlemage in Enshrouded 1.0

The 1.0 full release rewards hybrid players. Wand-focused characters got two new wand attacks and a crit-based bridge to staves. The new mage spells, especially Entangle and the summon totems, give the Battlemage crowd control it never had in early access.

The Battlemage holds up better than the pure Wizard because ward and shield parries refund mana and Life Burst adds sustain. You can push into Shroud zones and fight elite packs alone without a healer.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Battlemage after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Mage Apprentice, Wand Specialization, Wand Mastery.
  3. Put attribute points into Intelligence, Constitution, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Wand + ward or shield) before leaving base.

How to play the Battlemage build

  1. Start at mid range and hold the attack. Wand Mastery and Chain Hit do the work.
  2. When an elite swings, parry with your ward or shield for Arcane Deflection mana.
  3. Drop a totem or Entangle on packs before engaging.
  4. Dodge through attacks to fire Wand Evasion Attack discs into groups that line up.
  5. Fight from the air with the glider and Aerial Wand Attack when enemies swarm.

Enshrouded Battlemage build FAQ

Is the Battlemage wand build good in Enshrouded 1.0?

Yes. It is one of the best solo builds in 1.0. Unity and Arcane Deflection keep your mana topped up, and the new wand skills added more area damage.

Wand or staff for a Battlemage?

The wand is your main weapon. Carry a staff only if you plan to use Blast and Cast for burst windows.

Do I need a shield on a Battlemage?

You need an off-hand that can parry, but it does not have to be a shield. Wards are the mage off-hand in 1.0 and can block and parry too. Either one lets Arcane Deflection restore mana on a successful parry.

Ward or shield for a Battlemage?

Most 1.0 guides use a ward, with the Prismatic Ward the most common pick, because wards roll mana and magic stats. A shield blocks better. Choose the shield if you keep dying to melee packs.

What level do I get Unity?

Plan to reach it in the mid-game (around levels 16–35 in this path). The exact point depends on how many attribute nodes you take along the way.
