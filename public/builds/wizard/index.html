Enshrouded Wizard build overview

The Enshrouded Wizard build is still one of the strongest and most popular Enshrouded builds in the 1.0 full release. You stay at range and hit groups of enemies with fire, ice and lightning from a staff. When an enemy closes the distance you Blink away and swap to a wand to rebuild your mana. Enshrouded has no fixed classes, so "Wizard" here means a path through the blue Mage section of the skill tree, with nearly every point going into magic damage, magic critical chance and mana sustain.

Enshrouded 1.0 reset every skill tree and added nine new spells, so this mage build has been rebuilt around the new toolkit. Pillar of Flames, Blizzard and Ball Lightning give the Wizard real area denial. Mana Drain and Entangle cover utility, and the Eternal versions of spells no longer use up charges. This guide covers the full skill order from level 1 to the new level 50 cap, the attributes to raise, the gear to farm and how to play the build in solo and co-op.

What changed for the Wizard in Enshrouded 1.0

Before 1.0, mage players spent most of their time on wand basic attacks and used staves as finishers. The 1.0 changes push the build back toward real spellcasting. Blast and Cast now gives your next staff attack a 50% base damage bonus within 10 seconds of a wand critical hit, so the wand-to-staff swap became a real damage rotation. Aerial Wand Attack and Wand Evasion Attack also give you safer ways to fight while moving.

The raised level cap (45 → 50) and gear cap (55) also mean more points for both damage and survivability. The Wizard was always glass-cannon, so those extra points are best spent on Constitution and mana nodes, not more raw damage.

1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset at the 1.0 launch on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept.

Key Wizard skills explained

Blast and Cast

The new heart of the 1.0 Wizard rotation. Fish for a wand crit, swap to the staff and land the empowered hit.

Blink

Every mage build needs it. A teleport that makes kiting melee enemies and bosses much easier than rolling.

Unity

Mana sustain that pays you back for using the wand between spells. Without it you run dry in long fights.

Chain Hit

Turns the wand into a crowd-clearing tool and fills more mana through Unity procs.

How to play the Wizard build

  1. Open every fight from max range with Pillar of Flames or Blizzard on the densest group.
  2. Swap to the wand and spread Chain Hit shots across the pack to refill mana through Unity.
  3. When a wand crit lands, swap back to the staff right away to cash in Blast and Cast.
  4. Blink out whenever a melee enemy gets close. Never try to face-tank as a Wizard.
  5. On bosses, stand behind your tank in co-op, or kite in circles solo, and save Blizzard for add phases.

Wizard build pros and cons

Pros

  • Highest area damage in the game
  • Strong at every stage thanks to wand scaling
  • The new 1.0 spells added real crowd control
  • Very safe once Blink is unlocked

Cons

  • Low health pool. Mistakes are punished hard
  • Mana management takes practice
  • Gear-dependent: staff level matters a lot

Common wizard build mistakes

  • Putting every point into Intelligence and ignoring Constitution. At level 50, late-game enemies will one-shot you.
  • Spamming staff attacks until mana is gone instead of weaving wand attacks.
  • Leaving Eternal spells unupgraded. Their perks are a big part of 1.0 mage power.
  • Respeccing without re-checking your hotbar after the 1.0 skill tree reset.

Enshrouded Wizard build FAQ

Is the Wizard the best build in Enshrouded 1.0?

It is one of the top-tier builds. The Wizard has the best area damage in the game, and the nine new spells in 1.0 made it even stronger. A Barbarian or Tank is more forgiving for new players, though.

What attributes should an Enshrouded mage use?

Mostly Intelligence, then Spirit for mana. Add enough Constitution to survive late-game hits. Strength and Dexterity do nothing for a caster.

Did the 1.0 skill tree reset affect mage builds?

Yes. Every skill tree was reset at launch and the points were refunded. Re-spend them before leaving your base. The new mage skills include Advanced Necromancy, Totem Mastery, Aerial Wand Attack, Wand Evasion Attack and Blast and Cast.

Staff or wand for the Wizard build?

Both. The wand builds mana and triggers Blast and Cast, and the staff and spells deliver burst damage. Swapping between them is the core rotation.

What are the best spells for a Wizard in 1.0?

Pillar of Flames and Blizzard for area damage, Ball Lightning for moving targets, and Mana Drain for sustain. Upgrade the Eternal version of your main damage spell as soon as you can.
