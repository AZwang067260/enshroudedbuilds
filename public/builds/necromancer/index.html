Enshrouded Necromancer build overview

The Enshrouded Necromancer build is the newest archetype in the game and is getting a lot of searches after the 1.0 launch. 1.0 added four summoning spells (Summon Hollow Skeleton, Summon Fire Totem, Summon Ice Totem and Summon Shock Totem) and two new skills, Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery, which raise how many skeletons and totems you can keep active.

This summoner build takes those tools and puts them in a mage framework. Skeletons draw aggro and deal steady damage, totems hold chokepoints with elemental area damage, and you fire a wand from behind them. The guide explains how to path the new summon skills, which spells to slot, and how to keep your minions alive against 1.0 bosses.

What changed for the Necromancer in Enshrouded 1.0

Summons did not exist as a real playstyle before 1.0. Because Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery increase your summon limits, the Necromancer can now hold whole fights by itself. Solo players who struggled with aggro now have built-in tanks.

Since this is a brand-new archetype, exact summon numbers may still change with post-launch hotfixes. We will update this page when Keen Games publishes patch notes.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Necromancer after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Wizard / Battlemage entry nodes, Wand Mastery, Intelligence + Spirit nodes.
  3. Put attribute points into Intelligence, Spirit, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill tree section and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Wand + summon spells) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist.

Key Necromancer skills explained

Advanced Necromancy

More Hollow skeletons at once. More bodies means more damage and more aggro soaked.

Totem Mastery

More active totems. Mix Fire, Ice and Shock for damage plus slows.

Unity

Wand-based mana sustain so you can resummon all fight.

How to play the Necromancer build

  1. Summon skeletons before engaging and let them pull aggro.
  2. Drop an Ice Totem in the middle of the fight and a damage totem next to it.
  3. Stay behind your summons and fire the wand to refill mana through Unity.
  4. Resummon as soon as minions die. Don't let your frontline fall apart.
  5. On bosses, place totems where the boss will move to, not where it is now.

Necromancer build pros and cons

Pros

  • Built-in aggro control
  • Very relaxed solo play
  • New and fresh for 1.0
  • Strong in defensive base fights

Cons

  • Mana-hungry
  • Summon AI can pull extra packs
  • Lower single-target burst than a Wizard

Common necromancer build mistakes

  • Skipping Spirit and running out of mana mid-fight.
  • Placing totems out of range of the actual fight.
  • Forgetting summon limits. Take Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery.

Enshrouded Necromancer build FAQ

Is there a Necromancer class in Enshrouded?

There are no fixed classes, but Enshrouded 1.0 added Summon Hollow Skeleton, three elemental totem spells and the Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery skills. Together they make a real summoner build.

What does Advanced Necromancy do?

It increases how many skeletons you can have summoned at the same time.

What does Totem Mastery do?

It increases your active totem limit, so you can stack Fire, Ice and Shock totems together.

Is the summoner build good solo?

Yes. Skeletons soak aggro, which helps solo players most of all.
