Enshrouded 1.0 Necromancer / Summoner Build

The Enshrouded 1.0 summoner build: raise Hollow skeletons, plant elemental totems and let your army do the work, with a full skill order from level 1 to 50.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 necromancer build (also called the summoner build) puts points into Intelligence and Spirit, uses wand + summon spells and follows this skill order to level 50: Wizard + Mage Apprentice → Wand Mastery → Advanced Necromancy → Totem Mastery → Necromancer → Quick Charge → Mana Currents. Rated S-tier after the Update 1.0 skill tree reset (October 15, 2026).

Enshrouded Necromancer build overview

The Enshrouded Necromancer build is the newest archetype in the game and is getting a lot of searches after the 1.0 launch. 1.0 added four summoning spells (Summon Hollow Skeleton, Summon Fire Totem, Summon Ice Totem and Summon Shock Totem) and two new skills, Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery, which raise how many skeletons and totems you can keep active.

This summoner build takes those tools and puts them in a mage framework. Skeletons draw aggro and deal steady damage, totems hold chokepoints with elemental area damage, and you fire a wand from behind them. The guide explains how to path the new summon skills, which spells to slot, and how to keep your minions alive against 1.0 bosses.

What changed for the Necromancer in Enshrouded 1.0

Summons did not exist as a real playstyle before 1.0. Because Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery increase your summon limits, the Necromancer can now hold whole fights by itself. Solo players who struggled with aggro now have built-in tanks.

The 1.0 summoner core is consistent across the community: Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery for more summons, the Necromancer skill for a bonus skull companion, and the Trickster nodes Quick Charge and Mana Currents to keep casting. Exact percentages may still change with hotfixes.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Necromancer after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Wizard + Mage Apprentice, Wand Mastery, Intelligence + Spirit nodes.
  3. Put attribute points into Intelligence, Spirit, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (Wand + summon spells) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

Key Necromancer skills explained

Advanced Necromancy

Raises your Hollow skeleton cap from 1 to 4 at max rank. Over the cap, your oldest summon is replaced, so this skill is what turns one skeleton into an army.

Totem Mastery

Raises your totem cap from 1 to 4. Mix Fire (explodes when it expires), Ice (slows) and Shock (fills stun bars).

Necromancer

Magic-weapon kills can spawn a skull companion on top of your skeletons: 5% per rank, 15% at max.

Quick Charge &amp; Mana Currents

Two Trickster nodes that every 1.0 summoner guide takes: faster spell charging and more mana regen.

How to play the Necromancer build

  1. Summon skeletons before engaging and let them pull aggro.
  2. Drop an Ice Totem in the middle of the fight and a damage totem next to it.
  3. Stay behind your summons and fire the wand to refill mana through Unity.
  4. Resummon as soon as minions die. Don't let your frontline fall apart.
  5. On bosses, place totems where the boss will move to, not where it is now.

Common necromancer build mistakes

  • Skipping Spirit and running out of mana mid-fight.
  • Placing totems out of range of the actual fight.
  • Forgetting summon limits. Take Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery.

Enshrouded Necromancer build FAQ

What is the best necromancer skill order in Enshrouded 1.0?

Open with Wand Mastery and Intelligence/Spirit nodes (levels 1–15). In the mid game (16–35) take Advanced Necromancy, Totem Mastery, Necromancer, Quick Charge, Mana Currents and Unity. Finish with Constitution, Life Burst and Chain Hit (36–50). The full step-by-step table is in the skill order section above.

Is the necromancer build the same as the summoner build?

Yes. Players search for both names, but in Enshrouded 1.0 they mean the same build: a mage that fights with Summon Hollow Skeleton and the Fire, Ice and Shock totems, scaled by Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery.

Is there a Necromancer class in Enshrouded?

There are no fixed classes, but Enshrouded 1.0 added Summon Hollow Skeleton, three elemental totem spells and the Advanced Necromancy and Totem Mastery skills. Together they make a real summoner build.

What does Advanced Necromancy do?

It raises how many Hollow skeletons you can have at the same time by one per rank, from a cap of 1 up to 4 at max rank. It is one of the eight new skills in Enshrouded 1.0.

What does Totem Mastery do?

It raises your active totem limit by one per rank (up to 4 totems), so you can stack Fire, Ice and Shock totems together.

What does the Necromancer skill do?

Necromancer is a Mage-tree skill that gives magic kills a chance to summon a friendly skull companion. Game-file planners list a 5% chance per rank, 15% at three ranks.

Is the summoner build good solo?

Yes. Skeletons soak aggro, which helps solo players most of all.
