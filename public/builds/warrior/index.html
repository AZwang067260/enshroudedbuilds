Enshrouded 1.0 Warrior (One-Handed) Build

Sword, shield and steady sustain. The most beginner-friendly Enshrouded build and still strong at level 50.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 warrior build puts points into Strength and Constitution, uses one-handed sword/axe/mace + shield and follows this skill order to level 50: The Warrior's Path → Battle Heal → Veteran → Swift Blades → Vigorous Deflection → Blood Rage → Merciless Attack. Quick answer

Warrior: A-tier, easy difficulty, best for solo & co-op. It scales with Strength and Constitution and uses one-handed sword/axe/mace + shield. The level 50 planner preset spends 208 of 212 points. Core skills: Battle Heal, Swift Blades, Vigorous Deflection, Blood Rage, The Warrior's Path (+7 more). Main weakness: less burst than a Barbarian.

Enshrouded Warrior build overview

The Enshrouded Warrior build is the classic sword-and-board melee fighter. It is a balanced build that hits hard with one-handed weapons, blocks and parries with a shield, and heals itself through skills like Battle Heal. If you are new to Enshrouded or coming back after the 1.0 reset, this is the most forgiving way to learn combat.

In 1.0, dagger skills moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree, so this Warrior build focuses on swords, axes and maces. We use the core 1.0 Warrior skills (The Warrior's Path, Battle Heal, Veteran, Swift Blades, Vigorous Deflection and Blood Rage), the same core that most 1.0 sword-and-shield guides agree on, and lay out the best order to take them up to level 50.

What changed for the Warrior in Enshrouded 1.0

The 1.0 skill tree reset reduced costs on many existing skills and buffed several melee nodes, so the Warrior gets online faster than in early access. The higher level cap also leaves room for Tank defensive nodes on top of the full damage path.

Moving the dagger skills out also makes the Warrior tree simpler. You no longer waste points on dagger nodes you will never use.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Warrior after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Strength nodes, The Warrior's Path, Battle Heal.
  3. Put attribute points into Strength, Constitution, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (One-handed sword/axe/mace + shield) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

How to play the Warrior build

  1. Approach with your shield up and wait for the enemy's attack.
  2. Parry or block, then punish with a full light-attack combo.
  3. Let Battle Heal keep you topped up between enemies.
  4. Use power attacks on staggered enemies for Merciless Attack burst.
  5. Switch to the bow for flying enemies instead of chasing them.

Pros

Cons

  • Less burst than a Barbarian
  • Lots of melee contact in Shroud zones

  • Never parrying
  • Neglecting Endurance

Enshrouded Warrior build FAQ

Is the Warrior good in Enshrouded 1.0?

Yes. It is the most beginner-friendly build in 1.0 and stays effective up to the new level 50 cap thanks to Battle Heal and Vigorous Deflection.

Where did the dagger skills go in 1.0?

Dagger skills moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree in the 1.0 release.

Sword, axe or mace for the Warrior?

All of them work. Pick by attack speed and feel. Maces are great for staggering, and swords are the most consistent.

Warrior vs Barbarian: which is better?

The Barbarian deals more raw damage with two-handers. The Warrior is safer thanks to the shield and Battle Heal.
