Enshrouded 1.0 Tank / Guardian Build

Heavy plates, a big shield and the aggro of every enemy in the room. The anchor of any 1.0 co-op group.

The best Enshrouded 1.0 tank build (also called the guardian build) puts points into Constitution and Strength, uses one-handed mace + tower shield and follows this skill order to level 50: Heavy Plates → Battle Heal → Shiny Plates → Tower → Thick Skin → Nemesis → Warden. Rated A-tier after the Update 1.0 skill tree reset (October 15, 2026).

Quick answer

Tank: A-tier, easy difficulty, best for co-op (great solo too). It scales with Constitution and Strength and uses one-handed mace + tower shield. The level 50 planner preset spends 212 of 212 points. Core skills: Heavy Plates, Shiny Plates, Nemesis, Martyr, Earth Aura (+9 more). Main weakness: slowest kill speed.

Enshrouded Tank build overview

The Enshrouded Tank build stacks heavy armor, health and block value so you can stand in front of bosses while your team deals damage. The 1.0 tank builds focus on the Tank (Guardian) section of the Warrior tree: Heavy Plates and Shiny Plates for armor scaling, Tower and Warden for blocking, Nemesis for aggro, Earth Aura and Martyr for the group, and Thick Skin for raw survivability.

A Tank works well solo too. With a heavy mace, Battle Heal and Vigorous Deflection it can clear nearly anything slowly but safely. This page covers both versions: a pure co-op Guardian and a solo-capable Tank/Warrior hybrid.

What changed for the Tank in Enshrouded 1.0

The 1.0 level cap of 50 and gear tier 55 make boss damage spike, so groups need a dedicated frontline more than ever. Many Tank skills were also cheapened or buffed in the 1.0 pass, so you can fit both defense and real damage.

If returning players reset into Tank, they should know that heavy armor scaling skills multiply with the armor value of their gear. Upgrading armor to the new gear cap is worth more for a Tank than for any other build.

Update 1.0 skill tree reset

Every character's skill tree was reset when Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026, and all points were refunded. Re-spend your points before leaving your base. Your character level is kept. See all Enshrouded 1.0 builds.

How to re-spec the Tank after the 1.0 skill tree reset

  1. Stay in your base and open the skill tree. Every point was refunded when you first loaded your save in Update 1.0.
  2. Take the opening nodes first: Constitution nodes, Heavy Plates, Battle Heal (Warrior).
  3. Put attribute points into Constitution, Strength, following the priority table below.
  4. Follow the level 1–50 path in the skill order table and stop where your current level runs out.
  5. Re-check your hotbar and weapon swap (One-handed mace + tower shield) before leaving base.

For the changes that affect every build, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide and its full re-spec checklist, or the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes for every new skill and spell.

How to play the Tank build

  1. Engage first and hit every enemy once to build aggro with Nemesis.
  2. Hold block during telegraphed boss combos and parry the last hit.
  3. Rotate the boss so its back faces your Barbarian, Assassin or Wizard.
  4. Use Earth Aura when the group stacks up.
  5. Solo: fight slowly, block everything and let Battle Heal top you up.

Enshrouded Tank build FAQ

What is the best Tank build in Enshrouded 1.0?

A heavy-armor Guardian built around Heavy Plates, Shiny Plates, Thick Skin, Nemesis and Earth Aura, with Constitution as your main attribute and a strong shield.

Can you play a Tank solo in Enshrouded?

Yes. Take Battle Heal and Vigorous Deflection from the Warrior section. You will kill things more slowly but almost never die.

What armor should a Tank use?

The highest-level heavy armor set you can get. Heavy Plates and Shiny Plates scale with heavy armor.
