How builds work in Enshrouded

Enshrouded is a survival action RPG by Keen Games set in the Shroud-covered world of Embervale. It has no character classes in the traditional sense. Every Flameborn character uses the same large skill tree, and your build depends on where you spend your skill points, which attributes you raise, and which weapons, armor and spells you equip. When players search for Enshrouded builds, they usually want a proven path through that tree for a specific playstyle: a mage, a tank, an archer or a melee fighter.

The skill tree has twelve archetypes, grouped into three combat areas. The Warrior area holds the Warrior, Tank, Barbarian and Athlete. The Mage area holds the Wizard, Battlemage, Healer and Trickster. The Ranger area holds the Ranger, Assassin, Beastmaster and Survivor. Each archetype has its own passive bonuses and active skills, and the strongest builds usually go deep into one or two neighboring archetypes instead of spreading points thin. Our Enshrouded skill tree guide explains each archetype in detail.

Attributes are the second layer of every build. Strength scales melee damage, Dexterity scales bows and daggers, Intelligence scales staves, wands and spells, Constitution adds about 50 health per point, Spirit adds about 20 mana per point, and Endurance controls stamina for dodging, blocking and gliding. A good build picks one damage attribute and backs it up with the right mix of defensive stats.

Enshrouded 1.0 builds: what changed in the full release

Enshrouded left Early Access with the 1.0 release on October 15, 2026. This is the biggest balance change the game has had, and it is why so many players are searching for updated Enshrouded 1.0 builds. The headline changes for builds are:

  • Full skill tree reset for every character. Points are refunded and your level is kept.
  • Level cap raised from 45 to 50, and gear now goes up to level 55.
  • Eight new skills: five for mages (Advanced Necromancy, Totem Mastery, Aerial Wand Attack, Wand Evasion Attack, Blast and Cast) and three for archers (Puncture, Barrage, Survival Instinct).
  • Nine new spells, including summons: Mana Drain, Entangle, Pillar of Flames, Blizzard, Ball Lightning, Fire, Ice and Shock Totem and Hollow Wretch (a skeleton summon).
  • 22 Eternal spells and new Eternal arrows that can be upgraded and don't use up spell charges or ammo (Eternal arrows cost mana instead).
  • Dagger skills moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree, and many existing skills got cheaper or stronger.

Every guide on this site has been rewritten for these changes. For the full list with what each new skill and spell does, read the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes. For the full breakdown, and a checklist for re-spending your points after the reset, read our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide.

How to choose the right Enshrouded build

Solo or co-op?

Enshrouded supports up to 16 players on one server, and some builds are much better in groups. The Healer and the aggro-focused Tank are most useful with friends. Solo players should look at the Battlemage, the Warrior or the new Necromancer, because each one has its own sustain or aggro control.

Melee, ranged or magic?

If you like being in the middle of a fight, go Barbarian for damage or Warrior for safety. If you like to stay at range, choose between the Ranger for physical bow damage and the Wizard for elemental magic. If you want burst and mobility, the Assassin is the most demanding build and has the highest skill ceiling.

How much do you want to manage?

Mage builds have to manage mana, swap between wand and staff, and position carefully. Melee builds only track stamina. If you are new to Enshrouded, start with a Warrior and respec later. Skill points can be reset, and the 1.0 reset already gives everyone a fresh start.

Enshrouded builds FAQ

What are the best Enshrouded builds in 1.0?

The top Enshrouded 1.0 builds are the Battlemage wand build (best overall and best solo), the new Necromancer summoner build, the Wizard (best area damage) and the two-handed Barbarian (highest physical DPS). The Tank and Healer are the most valuable picks in co-op.

Does Enshrouded have classes?

No. Enshrouded uses one large skill tree with twelve archetypes: Warrior, Tank, Barbarian and Athlete; Wizard, Battlemage, Healer and Trickster; Ranger, Assassin, Beastmaster and Survivor. A "class build" is a path through these archetypes, and you can mix them freely. See Enshrouded classes explained.

When did Enshrouded 1.0 release?

Enshrouded 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026 on PC (Steam) and PlayStation 5, ending Early Access. An Xbox version is expected in 2027.

Did Enshrouded 1.0 reset my skills?

Yes. Every skill tree was reset at launch and all points were refunded. Your character level and items carry over, but you need to re-spend your points. Keen Games recommends doing this before leaving your base.

What is the max level in Enshrouded 1.0?

The player level cap went up from 45 to 50, and gear now goes up to level 55.

What is the best Enshrouded build for beginners?

The sword-and-shield Warrior. It has strong self-healing through Battle Heal, needs no mana management, and teaches you blocking and parrying.

What is the best solo build in Enshrouded?

The Battlemage wand build is the most reliable solo build in 1.0. The new Necromancer is a relaxed alternative, because summons absorb aggro for you.

Can I respec in Enshrouded?

Yes. Enshrouded lets you reset skill points, and the 1.0 launch already reset everyone for free. Our skill tree guide explains how points and resets work.
