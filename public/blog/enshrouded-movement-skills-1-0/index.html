Enshrouded 1.0 Movement Skills: Double Jump, Updraft, Gliding and More

Published November 2, 2026 by the Enshrouded Builds team

Enshrouded is an exploration game first, and the skills that get you around are the ones you use on every trip: jumping, gliding, sprinting and climbing. This post lists every movement skill in the 1.0 skill tree with its cost and in-game text from the game files (build 1267041), what each one actually does, and which of our build presets take it.

Short version: buy Double Jump first (2 points, and it sits right next to the starting Sparks). Add Airborne and Updraft for gliding once your build's core skills are done. Bow and dagger builds get the most from the ground skills, because Runner, Arachnoid and Silent Stride all sit in the Ranger area.

Double Jump and jump attacks

Double Jump is the one skill almost every build takes. It costs 2 points, sits in the Core area at the center of the tree, and each second jump costs 10 Stamina. It makes ledges, rooftops and dodging easier, and several attack skills build on it:

For melee builds, Crash Down: Force only works from a double jump, so it is a reason to keep Double Jump even on a heavy two-hander. Bow users get Bee Sting instead: aiming in mid-air slows your fall, which pairs well with a double jump over a crowd.

Gliding: Airborne and Updraft

Both gliding skills sit in the Assassin group of the Ranger area, so a mage or warrior has to path a little to reach them.

  • Airborne (1 pt × 3): Gliders consume 36% less Stamina. One rank is a cheap start; three ranks matter for long flights.
  • Updraft (4 pt): Jumping with [Jump] while gliding lifts you upwards. Can be used once per flight. Charge: As the Updraft charges before activation, the resulting lift is increased. A full charge grants you an additional boost. The charge is accelerated while inside your base. Cost: 100 Mana

Two details are easy to miss. Updraft costs Mana, not Stamina, so a glide never competes with your stamina bar. And it works once per flight: charge it fully for the extra boost instead of tapping it early.

Taking all three (Double Jump, Airborne and Updraft) from an empty tree costs at most 12 points, and less when the paths overlap. That's why every one of our level 50 presets includes Updraft.

Runner is the best of these for long trips: faster sprints that also cost less stamina. Arachnoid pays off if you climb cliffs instead of walking around them. Wanderlust and Splash Dash are niche, so take them only when your path passes them. Silent Stride is for stealth builds that open with a sneak attack.

Mage builds keep it light (Double Jump, one rank of Airborne and Updraft) because their points go into spells. Ranger and Assassin take nearly everything, since the movement skills share their area. Our skill points guide shows how many points you have at each stage if you want to fit more in.

What to buy first

  1. Double Jump as one of your first buys, for any build.
  2. Runner early if you play a bow or dagger build; it sits in your own area.
  3. Airborne (1 rank) and Updraft once your main damage skills are maxed, usually in the late game.
  4. Extra Airborne ranks, Arachnoid and the niche picks with leftover points.
