Enshrouded Battlemage: Wand Builds Before and After Update 1.0

Published October 24, 2026 by the Enshrouded Builds team

The wand was already the easiest way to play magic in Enshrouded, but before 1.0 it had one attack and a special ability that enemies could knock you out of. Update 1.0 gave it a full moveset and tied it to the staff. This post compares the Battlemage before and after 1.0, with skill values from the 1.0 game files (build 1267041) and quotes from Keen Games' 1.0 changelog.

Short version: wands now have a heavy attack from the start, two new attack skills (Wand Evasion Attack and Aerial Wand Attack), a staff combo (Blast and Cast), uninterruptible special abilities and much better targeting. Wards can now be repaired at camp anvils, and leech perks were rebalanced. The three new skills cost 10 points together.

What changed for wands in 1.0

  • A full moveset. "Wand gameplay has been expanded with a heavy attack that is immediately available, as well as unlockable evasive and aerial attacks as active skills."
  • No more interrupted specials. "Wand special abilities don't get interrupted by enemy attacks while casting anymore."
  • Targeting. Wand targeting "now better considers the maximum range of the projectile of each individual wand", hard-lock target switching with wands is "possible much faster after the attack", and "a new free aiming mode for wand gameplay has been added to the combat section in the gameplay settings". You can also target explosive barrels with wands now.
  • Wand and staff work together. The new Blast and Cast skill rewards switching to a staff right after a wand crit.

Wards, shields and leech perks

Two gear changes matter for a Battlemage who carries a ward or shield. First, "anvils found in enemy camps now finally repair equipped shields and wards", so long trips no longer end with a broken off-hand. Wards were also renamed for consistency, so don't be surprised if yours has a new name.

Second, Keen rebalanced leech: "In version 0.8 percentage based leech perks were too strong in the late game." Now "perks with leech now use a flat value" early on, and "the very valuable percentage leech is reserved for specific weapons and gear" in the late game. If your old build relied on percentage mana or health leech, expect less of it until you find those items. Unity and Arcane Deflection, which restore mana from the skill tree, matter more as a result.

The three new wand skills

SkillArea / groupCostIn-game text
Wand Evasion AttackMage / Battlemage4 ptWhen equipped with a Wand, you can perform a Wand Evasion Attack by pressing [Use Item] while evading. It fires a magical disc that pierces enemies and deals 50% additional weapon damage. Cost: 50 Mana
Aerial Wand AttackMage / Battlemage3 ptWhen equipped with a Wand, you can perform a special attack by holding [Use Item] during a jump. It fires a magical projectile that deals 50% additional weapon damage in a small blast radius. Cost: 25 Stamina
Blast and CastMage / Trickster3 ptAfter a Critical Strike with a Wand, the base damage of your next Staff attack within 10 seconds is increased by 50%.

Wand Evasion Attack is the best of the three for a Battlemage: you dodge all the time anyway, and now every dodge fires a piercing disc. Aerial Wand Attack is cheap utility. Blast and Cast sits in the Trickster group and pays off if you carry a staff as your second weapon. The new 1.0 skills post compares all eight.

How to skill a Battlemage in 1.0

Wand Evasion Attack costs 8 points from an empty tree by the cheapest path, and our planner preset already has all three new wand skills by the end of the levels 1–15 stage (65 points). The full core of our Battlemage preset: Mage Apprentice, Wand Specialization, Wand Mastery, Arcane Proliferation, Sting, Unity, Arcane Deflection, Life Burst, Chain Hit, Wand Evasion Attack, Aerial Wand Attack, Blink, Blast and Cast, Wizard, Exalted, Power Parry, Double Jump.

Load the level 1–15 Battlemage preset or the full level 50 build in the planner. The Battlemage build guide has the complete skill order, wands, wards and spells, and the Mage skill tree page lists every skill in the area.

Is the Battlemage still the best build in 1.0?

For most players, yes. Our tier list keeps it at S-tier and calls it the easiest strong solo build: the ward covers mistakes, and Unity and Arcane Deflection keep the mana flowing even with weaker leech. Its main trade-off: lower burst than a pure wizard. If you want that burst, look at the Wizard, or read how the Necromancer and Ranger changed in 1.0.
