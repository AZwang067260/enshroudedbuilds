Enshrouded Ranger: Bow Builds Before and After Update 1.0

Published October 23, 2026 by the Enshrouded Builds team

The bow was a solid choice in Early Access, but it came with chores: crafting stacks of arrows, repairing bows and juggling targets. Update 1.0 fixed most of that and added three new archer skills. This post compares the Ranger before and after 1.0, with skill values from the 1.0 game files (build 1267041) and quotes from Keen Games' 1.0 changelog.

Short version: 1.0 gave bows upgradeable Eternal arrows, three new skills (Puncture, Barrage and Survival Instinct, 12 points together), better aiming and target switching, and reworked ranged armor. The bow build itself plays the same: crits and weak-point shots.

What changed for bows in 1.0

  • Eternal arrows. The changelog "added new eternal ember arrows and eternal voltaic arrows at various tiers", and "Eternal spells, and arrows, can now be upgraded just like weapons or other equipment." The crafting survivors for each combat class now offer those upgrades.
  • Arrow balance. Keen "revisited the balancing of the various arrows to make sure costs and effectiveness provide better options for rangers", and reduced the ectoplasm needed in arrow recipes.
  • Aiming and targeting. "Switching targets with wands and bows in hard-lock is now possible much faster after the attack." Ranged and melee weapons now have separate auto-target settings, and controller aim assist for ranged weapons was improved.
  • Armor. The ranger armor sets for the Revelwood and Nomad Highlands tiers were reworked: "both the tier 2 and 3 have one craftable ranged combat set and one craftable dagger set each", while the Marksman and Sharpshooter sets became loot from those biomes' chests.
  • A boss change to know. The weak point on the back of the Albaneve Summits boss "now works as a regular weak point for all weapons; previously, it only worked as a weak point for arrows." Bows lost a unique edge there, though the changelog adds that stun arrows now work "significantly better than before" on that fight.

The three new archer skills

All three sit in the Ranger area. Values are the in-game text at each rank:

Survival InstinctRanger / Survivor1 pt × 3Rank 1: Defeating a Shroud infested enemy with a Bow or Dagger restores 3 seconds of Time in the Shroud.

Puncture is the one that matters most: a flat bonus to ranged Piercing damage with no condition attached. Barrage is quality of life: bow hits can restore durability, which means fewer repair trips. Survival Instinct gives back Shroud time for Shroud kills with a bow or dagger, which is worth more now that stacked Shroud time bonuses are weaker. How Multi Shot and Skill Shot work in 1.0

These three skills cause the most confusion, so here is the full in-game text at max rank:

  • Multi Shot (4 pt): Shooting Regular Arrows has a 20% chance to spawn a Flurry of Arrows that spreads slightly. Regular Arrows fired this way will subtract from your ammunition. Flurry of Arrows does not trigger on Special Arrows.
  • Multi Shot Trigger (3 pt): Shooting Special Arrows now triggers a Flurry of Arrows from the Multi Shot skill. Special Arrows fired this way will subtract from your ammunition.
  • Skill Shot (1 pt × 3): Skillshot damage is increased by 60%. Skillshot: Striking enemy weak points, such as their heads, with Arrows is considered a Skillshot. Skillshots deal 100% increased base damage against enemies without helmets and 50% against helmeted enemies. It can be further increased by the Skillshot damage attribute.

In short: Multi Shot only fires extra arrows from regular arrows, and those arrows come out of your ammo. Multi Shot Trigger extends that to special arrows, which also use ammo. Skill Shot rewards weak-point hits, especially on enemies without helmets.

How to skill a Ranger in 1.0

Maxing Puncture from an empty tree costs 13 points by the cheapest path, and our planner preset already has all three new archer skills at max rank by the end of the levels 1–15 stage (65 points). The full core of our Ranger preset: Ranger, Marksman, Bow Specialization, Skill Shot, Sniper, Puncture, Blessed Arrows, Fatal Precision, Multi Shot, Barrage, Survival Instinct, Graceful Stride, Double Jump.

Open the level 1–15 Ranger preset or the full level 50 build in the planner. The Ranger build guide has the complete skill order, bows, arrows and armor, and the Ranger skill tree page lists every skill in the area by group.

Is the Ranger worth playing in 1.0?

Yes, if you enjoy aiming. Our tier list puts the Ranger at A-tier: Eternal arrows and Puncture removed most of the old friction, but the build is still aim-dependent and weaker in tight spaces than melee or magic. Many players pair it with daggers: since 1.0 the dagger skills sit in the same area, which makes the Assassin a natural hybrid.
