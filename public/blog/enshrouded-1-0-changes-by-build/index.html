Enshrouded 1.0 Patch Notes Explained: What Changed for Each Build

Published October 25, 2026 by the Enshrouded Builds team

The 1.0 changelog is long, and most of it is about the world, quests and building. This post pulls out only the lines that change how a build plays, and sorts them by build. Quotes are from Keen Games' 1.0 changelog (as published on the Enshrouded Wiki); skill costs are from the 1.0 game files (build 1267041). For the full list of what's new, see our Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes summary.

Short version: mages gained the most (new spells, Eternal spells, wand attacks, summons), melee got a smoother stamina and blocking loop, bows lost most of their ammo chores, and daggers moved to their own tree. Two changes hit everyone: the skill tree reset and the Shroud time rework.

Changes that hit every build

  • Skill tree reset, level 50. Every old character's tree was reset on first load, and a fully explored level 50 character now has 212 skill points. The numbers are in what changed in the 1.0 skill tree.
  • Shroud time. Keen found that stacking Shroud time effects pushed the timer "very far into the background", so "their overall effectiveness has been reduced", while "many more hourglass capsules" now appear. Don't spend many points on Shroud time skills.
  • Leech perks. Percentage leech "was too strong in the late game". Early perks now use a flat value, and "the very valuable percentage leech is reserved for specific weapons and gear."
  • Enemy resistances. "Enemy damage susceptibilities have been reworked for a large number of enemies", and the "Effective" and "Resisted" labels now only show at 20% or more. Matching your damage type to the enemy pays off more consistently.
  • Boss retries. Enemies now recover health over time after you leave combat instead of resetting instantly, so a quick retry after a wipe starts with a partly damaged boss.
  • Gems. Gem of Battle Mania and Gem of Obsession had "some stats" lowered, and Gem of Toxic Reaction got "+21% dps on level 10 up to 65% on level 50."
  • Armor. Each Flame upgrade now unlocks the next tier's armor sets automatically.

Mage builds: spells, wands and summons

The changelog "added 9 new types of spells", "numerous additional tiers for previously existing spells", and says "all spells now have an eternal version". Eternal spells and arrows can be upgraded at the combat crafting survivors. Spell charges got cheaper: rare resources were removed from recipes, mined gems were added, and less ectoplasm is needed. That helps the Wizard and Healer most.

The Battlemage got its own rework: a heavy attack, specials that "don't get interrupted by enemy attacks while casting anymore", range-aware targeting and a free-aim mode. Details in Battlemage before and after 1.0.

The Necromancer came with limits. The Gem of Lesser Necromancy "now has a limit for the resurrected companions it can spawn", gem companions "start to lose health after a while when out of combat", and the game now "counts the currently active creatures spawned by players and enforces a maximum." On the plus side, skeleton companions' attacks "are no longer interrupted by enemy attacks." More in Necromancer before and after 1.0.

Melee builds: blocking, stamina and heavy attacks

  • "The player character's stamina now also recovers while blocking." This is the biggest change for the Warrior and Tank.
  • Blocking now uses "the camera blocking direction, rather than the direction the character is facing", which "should make blocking feel more responsive."
  • "Heavy melee attacks can now hit several enemies when they are close to each other", and Keen "fixed the attack ranges for two-handed axes". Both help the Barbarian.
  • The Reaping weapon perk was "greatly buffed": its stamina regeneration "was previously too short to make a noticeable difference in combat."
  • Anvils in enemy camps "now finally repair equipped shields and wards."
  • Melee auto-targeting now "more strongly prefers enemies that are in close proximity to the player."

Ranger and Assassin: arrows and daggers

For the Ranger, Keen "revisited the balancing of the various arrows", "added new eternal ember arrows and eternal voltaic arrows", and made target switching with bows "possible much faster after the attack". The Albaneve Summits boss's back weak point "now works as a regular weak point for all weapons; previously, it only worked as a weak point for arrows." See Ranger before and after 1.0.

For the Assassin, the dagger skills moved "from the warrior area of the skill tree to the assassin tree". Tiers 2 and 3 now each have "one craftable ranged combat set and one craftable dagger set". The dagger sets Assassin, Frostbite Slasher and Blood Feather now have "a melee attack critical hit chance improvement upgrade perk instead of a sneak attack damage increase."

What to do with your build now

  1. Spend your refunded points. An empty tree is the biggest power loss in 1.0.
  2. Don't rebuy your old build node for node: costs changed and some skills moved.
  3. Plan in the skill planner first. It has every build at level 15, 35 and 50, and you can enter your in-game point total.
  4. Check the 1.0 tier list if you're choosing a new build, and the respec guide if you need to change one later.
