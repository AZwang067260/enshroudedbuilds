Enshrouded Patch 1.0.1: What Changed for Your Build

Published November 9, 2026 by the Enshrouded Builds team

Keen Games released Patch #1 (1.0.1.0) on November 2, 2026, the first update with balance changes since Enshrouded left Early Access. Two hotfixes followed on November 3 and 4. This post goes through the changelog and keeps only what matters for your build, with quotes from Keen's notes.

Short version: wand builds got a nerf and two buffs, two-handed builds got a cheaper Whirlwind Crescendo, and ranged builds can finally fight bosses from a distance without the boss healing. The patch doesn't change any skill's point cost or place in the tree, so you don't need to respec.

The three skill changes

These are the only skill changes in the notes. "Before" is the cost from the 1.0 launch game data; "after" is what the changelog says:

SkillBefore (1.0 launch data)After Patch 1.0.1.0
Aerial Wand Attack25 Stamina40 Mana
Wand Evasion Attack50 ManaLess mana (the changelog gives no number)
Whirlwind Crescendo75 StaminaLess stamina (the changelog gives no number)

Wand builds: one nerf, two buffs

  • No more animation cancelling. "Wand attack frequency can't be unnaturally boosted anymore by animation-cancelling each shot via blocking." If you blocked between shots to fire faster, your wand damage per second goes down. Normal play is unchanged.
  • Aerial Wand Attack now costs mana. It "now costs 40 mana instead of stamina". Your stamina is free for dodging and jumping, but the jump shot now competes with your spells for mana.
  • Wand Evasion Attack is cheaper. Keen "reduced the mana costs for the Wand Evasion Attack". It was already the best of the new wand skills, and now you can fire it more often.

For the Enshrouded Battlemage build, the net effect is positive: the two skills it relies on for mobile damage got cheaper or moved off stamina. The Wizard uses the wand mostly to refill mana, so only the animation-cancel fix touches it.

Ranged builds: bosses stop healing at range

"Bosses now stay engaged in combat longer after having been attacked and even when players are far away." Keen's own example: ranged fights against the Fell Monstrosity were frustrating "when the boss suddenly started to recover health even while being shot at." That was a problem for every build that fights at range, so Ranger, Wizard and summoner players benefit most. The patch also shrank the hitbox of Fell mini critters, which "could block arrows when trying to shoot enemies behind them."

Melee builds: cheaper Whirlwind Crescendo

Keen "lowered the stamina costs for Whirlwind Crescendo", the two-handed whirl at the end of an attack chain (75 Stamina in the launch data). Two-handed builds such as the Barbarian can use it more often without running dry. Lupa's scalper daggers also enable the riposte kick again, which matters for dagger builds that use them.

Summoners and healers

  • The Wispwyvern and its undead version "couldn't get targeted correctly by totem spells and other summons". That's fixed, so a summoner build works against them now. See how to summon skeletons and totems.
  • "Fixed that the Chain Heal spell can also heal enemy Hollow skeletons." Healers no longer patch up the enemy by accident.

Fixes for every build

  • "Fixed that skills and attacks could stop working correctly after playing for several hours." If a skill seemed to stop working in long sessions, this was probably why.
  • Fewer Fell enemies drop no loot ("another fix for this is planned for the next update"), and elite flying critters give more XP.
  • Sprint can now be set to toggle in the keyboard and mouse settings.
  • Hotfixes 1.0.1.1 and 1.0.1.2 only fix crashes, sound, textures and pinned recipes. They list no balance changes.

Do you need to respec after 1.0.1?

No. No skill moved and no point cost changed, so your tree is still valid. The only reason to change it is if the new costs change your mind: a Battlemage who skipped Wand Evasion Attack may want it now. Check the change in the Enshrouded skill planner before you pay Runes at the Flame Altar.
