Enshrouded 1.0 Patch Notes: Every New Skill, Spell & Change

Enshrouded Update 1.0 launched on October 15, 2026. Here is every change that matters for your character: the skill tree reset, the level 50 cap, gear 55, 8 new skills, 9 new spells, Eternal gear and the dagger move.

Enshrouded 1.0 update at a glance

Unofficial summary

This is a player-focused summary of the 1.0 changes that affect skills, spells and builds. It is not the full official changelog. For every fix and balance number, check Keen Games' official patch notes on Steam.

Skill tree reset in 1.0

The biggest change for existing characters: every skill tree was reset. All skill points are refunded the first time you load your save in Update 1.0. Your character level, items and bases stay. Keen Games' own advice is to re-spend your points before leaving your base. Many existing skills also got lower costs or stronger effects, so old builds are worth re-planning instead of rebuilding point for point.

Not sure how to spend them? Our Enshrouded 1.0 builds guide has a re-spec checklist, and every class guide has a level 1–50 skill order.

Level cap 50 and gear level 55

The player level cap rose from 45 to 50, which means more skill points for every build: 212 in total at level 50 according to community planners built from the game files: 98 from levels (2 per level after level 1), 60 from Shroud Roots (1 each) and 54 from Elixir Wells (3 each). You only reach 212 if you explore every biome. Gear now goes up to level 55, so the top weapons and armor sets of Early Access are no longer the best in the game. Upgrading your main weapon to the new tier is the biggest damage increase after the update.

Eternal spells and Eternal arrows

1.0 adds Eternal versions of spells: 22 tier-7 Eternal spells that cast without using up spell charges and can be upgraded with perks. Archers get upgradeable Eternal arrows (an Eternal Ember fire line unlocks at levels 15, 28 and 45, plus a shock line) that cost mana instead of arrows, which removes most of the arrow crafting grind. See the Wizard and Ranger guides for which ones to upgrade first.

Wand heavy attack and combat changes

Keen Games' official combat post confirms that wands now have a heavy attack that pierces through enemies and can interrupt weaker foes. Lock-on targeting was improved, wands can now hit explosive barrels, and melee heavy attacks now hit multiple enemies. Mana costs and cast times of spells were rebalanced as well.

Dagger skills moved to the Assassin tree

Dagger skills moved from the Warrior tree to the Assassin tree. Dagger players can now focus their points in one area, and Warriors no longer path around dagger nodes. Read the updated Assassin build and Warrior build.

What to do after updating to 1.0

  1. Load your save and stay in your base. Your skill points have been refunded.
  2. Pick a build from the Enshrouded 1.0 tier list and follow its skill order.
  3. Slot at least one new spell or Eternal arrow if you play a mage or archer.
  4. Farm toward the new level 55 gear tier.

Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes FAQ

When were the Enshrouded 1.0 patch notes released?

Update 1.0 went live on October 15, 2026 on PC (Steam) and PlayStation 5, ending Early Access. The Xbox version is planned for 2027.

What are the new skills in Enshrouded 1.0?

Eight new skills: five for mages (Advanced Necromancy, Totem Mastery, Aerial Wand Attack, Wand Evasion Attack, Blast and Cast) and three for archers (Puncture, Barrage, Survival Instinct).

What are the new spells in Enshrouded 1.0?

Nine new spells: Mana Drain, Entangle, Pillar of Flames, Blizzard, Ball Lightning, Summon Fire Totem, Summon Ice Totem, Summon Shock Totem and Summon Hollow Skeleton.

Did Enshrouded 1.0 reset my skill tree?

Yes. Every character's skill tree was reset and all skill points were refunded the first time you load your save in 1.0. Your level, items and base are kept.

What is the new level cap in Enshrouded 1.0?

The player level cap went up from 45 to 50, and gear now goes up to level 55. A level 50 character has 212 skill points in total.

Does Enshrouded 1.0 have crossplay?

No, there is no crossplay at launch.
