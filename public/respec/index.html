How to Respec in Enshrouded 1.0

Reset your skill tree at a Flame Altar for 1 Rune per skill point you have spent. Here is how it works, what it costs at each stage of the game, and how to plan the new build so you only pay once.

Plan your new buildRespec cost

How to reset your skill tree

  1. Plan the new build first. Build it in the skill planner, which uses the real 1.0 tree, costs and connections. Copy the link so you can follow it in game.
  2. Collect enough Runes. The reset costs 1 Rune per skill point you have spent (see the table below).
  3. Go to a Flame Altar and choose the option to reset your skill tree. All spent points come back to you.
  4. Re-buy your plan point by point, starting from the nodes nearest your new main skills.

How much a respec costs

The Enshrouded Wiki's Flame Altar page says the altar "allows players to reset their skill tree at a cost of 1 Runes per spent skill point". So the cost grows with your character. With the point budgets our guides use for each stage of the game:

If you reset at the end ofPoints spent (fully explored)Reset cost
Levels 1–1565 points65 Runes
Levels 16–35140 points140 Runes
Levels 36–50212 points212 Runes

If you haven't cleared every Shroud Root and Elixir Well, you have fewer points spent and the reset costs less. The planner shows your exact total when you set your level and explored biomes.

The free 1.0 reset

Every character that existed before 1.0 got one reset for free. The changelog's "public safety announcement" reads: "When playing for the first time in version 1.0.0.0 with an existing save game, the skill tree will be reset. Please make sure to spend the skill points again!" If you haven't re-spent those points yet, you don't need the altar: just spend them. Many skills changed cost, effect or position in 1.0, and the dagger skills moved to the Assassin group, so don't rebuy your old build node for node. What changed in the 1.0 skill tree lists the changes.

Respec tips

  • Reset once, not often. The cost goes up with every point you own, so late-game resets are the most expensive.
  • Pick one main archetype. The whole tree costs 675 points and you get 212 at most. See the skill tree guide and the area pages for the Mage, Warrior, Ranger and Core skills.
  • Re-check your hotbar and weapon swaps after you re-buy your skills, so they match the new build.
  • Coming back to the game after 1.0? Follow the 1.0 re-spec checklist.

Respec FAQ

How do you respec in Enshrouded?

At a Flame Altar. According to the Enshrouded Wiki, the altar lets you reset your skill tree at a cost of 1 Rune per skill point you have spent. The reset refunds your points so you can spend them again.

How much does a respec cost in Enshrouded 1.0?

1 Rune per spent skill point. A fully explored level 50 character with all 212 points spent pays 212 Runes; at level 15 with two biomes explored it's 65.

Did Enshrouded 1.0 reset my skill tree?

Yes, once and for free. The 1.0 changelog says: "When playing for the first time in version 1.0.0.0 with an existing save game, the skill tree will be reset. Please make sure to spend the skill points again!"

How can I avoid paying for a respec twice?

Plan the whole new build first in our free skill planner, which follows the game's rules for connections and costs, then reset once and buy it point by point.
