Enshrouded Warrior Skill Tree (1.0)

The Warrior area is the melee half of the tree: one-handed weapons and sustain in the Warrior group, heavy armor, blocking and aggro in Tank, two-handed weapons in Barbarian, and stamina and movement in Athlete. 1.0 moved the dagger skills out of this area, so the paths are cleaner for sword, axe and hammer users.

The Warrior area at a glance

The Warrior area has 63 nodes and 43 different skills. Maxing every one of them would cost 183 points, which is 86% of the 212 points a fully explored level 50 character has. You could fill it, but most builds only take the few skills they need.

What's new in the Warrior skill tree in 1.0

No new combat skill landed in the Warrior area, but 1.0 moved all dagger skills to the Ranger area's Assassin group, lowered the cost of many skills and repositioned Veteran and One-Handed Specialization. Heavy melee attacks now hit several enemies, and stamina recovers while blocking.

Where to start in the Warrior area

A few nodes can be learned without owning a neighbour. They are the entry points of the Warrior area:

  • Constitution node (1 pt) leads to Heavy Plates
  • Constitution node (1 pt) leads to The Warrior's Path
  • Strength node (1 pt) leads to Barbarian's Path
  • Strength node (1 pt) leads to Wet Dog

From there, a node unlocks once it connects to one you already have. Use the planner to see the cheapest path to any skill.

Warrior leveling route: level 1 to 50

Every build in this area follows the same three stages. Points come from levels and from the Shroud Roots and Elixir Wells you clear, so each stage below assumes you have explored the biomes that match your level. Each link opens that stage's preset in the planner:

BuildStage 1Stage 2Stage 3
BarbarianLevels 1–15 (65 pt)Levels 16–35 (140 pt)Levels 36–50 (212 pt)
WarriorLevels 1–15 (65 pt)Levels 16–35 (140 pt)Levels 36–50 (212 pt)
TankLevels 1–15 (65 pt)Levels 16–35 (140 pt)Levels 36–50 (212 pt)

The general rule: in stage 1 take your weapon's specialization and the first damage nodes of your main group, in stage 2 buy and max the build-defining skills, and in stage 3 finish capstones and reach into one neighbouring group for defense or sustain. Each class guide explains why its skills come in that order.

Warrior skill tree FAQ

How many skills are in the Enshrouded Warrior skill tree?

The Warrior area of the 1.0 tree has 63 nodes and 43 different skills in 4 groups (Tank, Warrior, Barbarian, Athlete). Maxing every node would cost 183 skill points, against a budget of 212 at level 50.

What is new in the Warrior skill tree in 1.0?

No new combat skill was added to this area. No new combat skill landed in the Warrior area, but 1.0 moved all dagger skills to the Ranger area's Assassin group, lowered the cost of many skills and repositioned Veteran and One-Handed Specialization. Heavy melee attacks now hit several enemies, and stamina recovers while blocking.

Where do you start in the Warrior area?

From its starting nodes, which you can learn without owning a neighbour: Constitution node (1 pt); Constitution node (1 pt); Strength node (1 pt); Strength node (1 pt).

Which builds use the Warrior skill tree?

Our Barbarian, Warrior, Tank builds spend most of their points in the Warrior area.
