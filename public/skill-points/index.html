How Many Skill Points in Enshrouded 1.0?

A fully explored level 50 character has 212 skill points: 98 from levels and 114 from Shroud Roots and Elixir Wells. Below: where every point comes from, how many you have at each level, and what 212 points can buy.

Plan your pointsPoints by level

Where skill points come from

Enshrouded has three sources of skill points. All numbers on this page come from the 1.0 game files (build 1267041).

  • Levels: 2 points for every level after 1. Level 50 is the 1.0 cap, so that's 98 points.
  • Shroud Roots: 1 point each. There are 60, worth 60 points.
  • Elixir Wells: 3 points each. There are 18, worth 54 points.

That adds up to 98 + 60 + 54 = 212. More than half of your points come from exploring, not from levels.

Our build guides use three of these as stages:

  • Levels 1–15: 65 points with 2 biomes explored
  • Levels 16–35: 140 points with 4 biomes explored
  • Levels 36–50: 212 points with 7 biomes explored

Not sure how many Roots you have? Your in-game skill tree shows the exact total. Type it into the skill planner under Explored biomes and it will use your real budget.

What 212 skill points can buy

The 1.0 tree has 231 nodes and costs 675 points to fill, so even a fully explored character can afford about 31% of it:

Filling the Mage or Ranger area alone takes more than your whole budget. That's why every one of our 1.0 builds goes deep in one area and borrows a few nodes from a neighbour. The skill tree guide explains the archetypes inside each area.

Why you might have fewer than 212

  • Missed Shroud Roots. 1.0 added Roots in new areas at the edge of the map, which are easy to skip.
  • Uncleansed Elixir Wells. Each one is 3 points, so one missed Well costs as much as three Roots.
  • Not level 50 yet. Each level is 2 points.

Respecs don't cost points: a reset at a Flame Altar refunds everything for 1 Rune per spent point. See how to respec.

Skill points FAQ

How many skill points are there in Enshrouded 1.0?

212 at most: 98 from levels 2 to 50, plus 114 from 60 Shroud Roots and 18 Elixir Wells.

How many skill points do you get per level?

2 for every level after level 1, so 98 by the level 50 cap.

How many skill points does an Elixir Well give?

3 skill points. A Shroud Root gives 1.

Does the Flame level give skill points?

No. In the 1.0 game data each Flame level adds attribute points, not skill points. Skill points only come from levels, Shroud Roots and Elixir Wells.

Can you max out the skill tree in Enshrouded?

No. The whole tree costs 675 points and you get 212, about 31% of it. Every build picks a path through one area.

Why do I have fewer than 212 skill points at level 50?

You are missing Shroud Roots or Elixir Wells. 60 Roots and 18 Wells exist, including 6 Roots that 1.0 added in the new areas near the edge of the map.
